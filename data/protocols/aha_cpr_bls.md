# AHA Guidelines for CPR & ECC / Indian Resuscitation Council BLS

**Authority**: American Heart Association & Indian Resuscitation Council (IRC)  
**Source**: 2020 AHA Guidelines for CPR and Emergency Cardiovascular Care  
**Statutory Shield**: Section 134A Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019  

## Adult Basic Life Support (BLS) & CPR
- **Step 1: Check Responsiveness**: Tap shoulder and shout. Check for absent or abnormal/gasping breathing (<10 sec).
- **Step 2: Emergency Activation**: Call 108 immediately and send for an AED.
- **Step 3: Chest Compressions (110 BPM)**: Push hard and fast in center of chest (lower sternum) at 100–120 BPM cadence (110 BPM target). Compress 2–2.4 inches (5–6 cm) deep. Allow complete recoil.
- **Step 4: AED Deployment**: Apply pads to upper right and lower left chest. Follow voice prompts. Clear patient before shock and immediately resume compressions.
- **Contraindications**: Do not give oral water or medication. Minimize compression interruptions to under 10 seconds.
