# Indian Red Cross Society & IFRC Emergency Response Protocols

**Authority**: Indian Red Cross Society & MoHFW, Govt of India  
**Source**: Red Cross First Aid Manual & National Snakebite Protocol  
**Statutory Shield**: Section 134A Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019  

## Venomous Snakebite First Response (MoHFW §3)
- **Step 1: Strict Immobilization**: Reassure victim and enforce complete body rest. Physical movement accelerates venom dissemination through the lymphatic system.
- **Step 2: Pressure Immobilization Technique (PIT)**: Wrap bitten limb snugly with a wide elastic crepe bandage and apply a rigid splint at or below heart level.
- **Step 3: Remove Constrictive Items**: Remove rings, bangles, anklets, and shoes before swelling develops.
- **Step 4: Immediate 108 ALS Transfer**: Transfer immediately to a hospital with Polyvalent Anti-Snake Venom (ASV) and ventilator facilities.
- **Contraindications**: Absolutely NO arterial tourniquets, NO cutting or incising the fang marks, NO sucking venom, and NO herbal oral concoctions.

## Convulsive Seizures & Status Epilepticus
- **Step 1: Environmental Safety**: Move sharp objects and cushion victim's head.
- **Step 2: Airway Protection**: Do not force anything between teeth. Do not restrain limbs during convulsions.
- **Step 3: Time Tracking**: Time the seizure. If jerking lasts > 5 minutes, alert 108 for status epilepticus.
- **Step 4: Recovery Position**: Roll victim onto side in lateral recovery position once spasms stop.
- **Contraindications**: Strictly nothing inside the mouth (no spoons, keys, fingers, or liquids).

## Acute Stroke Triage (FAST Protocol)
- **Step 1: FAST Screening**: Check Face drooping, Arm weakness, Slurred speech, and exact Time of onset.
- **Step 2: Emergency Center**: Dispatch ALS ambulance to a certified stroke center for IV thrombolysis within 4.5 hours.
- **Step 3: Positioning**: Elevate head 30 degrees.
- **Contraindications**: Nil by mouth. Never administer aspirin before brain CT scan rules out hemorrhagic stroke.
