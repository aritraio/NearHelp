# WHO Guidelines for Essential Trauma Care & Basic Life Support

**Authority**: World Health Organization & American College of Surgeons  
**Source**: WHO Essential Trauma Care Series  
**Statutory Shield**: Section 134A Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019  

## Severe Bleeding & Hemorrhage Control (Guideline 4.1)
- **Step 1: Direct Pressure**: Immediately apply direct firm pressure over the bleeding site with clean cloth or gloved hands. Maintain continuous uninterrupted pressure for at least 5 minutes.
- **Step 2: Deep Wound Packing**: For deep junctional wounds in the groin or axilla, pack tightly with hemostatic or sterile gauze.
- **Step 3: Arterial Tourniquet**: For life-threatening extremity hemorrhage, place a commercial windlass tourniquet 2–3 inches proximal to the wound (avoiding joints) and tighten until arterial bleeding stops. Record the timestamp.
- **Contraindications**: Do not give oral water or food (NO_ORAL_FLUIDS). Never probe, clamp, or cut inside bleeding wounds (NO_SURGICAL_EXPLORATION).

## Acute Airway Obstruction & Choking (Guideline 2.4)
- **Step 1: Assessment**: If victim cannot breathe or speak, begin immediate intervention.
- **Step 2: Back Blows**: Deliver 5 sharp back blows between shoulder blades.
- **Step 3: Abdominal Thrusts**: Deliver 5 upward abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver) below ribcage.
- **Step 4: Unconscious CPR**: If victim collapses, begin CPR compressions at 110 BPM. Inspect mouth before breaths.
- **Contraindications**: No blind finger sweeps. No surgical tracheotomy in field conditions.

## Thermal Burns & Scalds (Guideline 7.2)
- **Step 1: Extinguish & Remove**: Stop burning process immediately.
- **Step 2: 20-Minute Water Cooling**: Cool burn wound under clean running tap water (15–20°C) for 20 continuous minutes.
- **Step 3: Dressing**: Cover loosely with transparent plastic cling film or sterile non-adherent dressing.
- **Contraindications**: Never apply ice, butter, grease, or toothpaste. Do not pull off clothes stuck to burned skin.
