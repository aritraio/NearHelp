# National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) Response Guidelines

**Authority**: National Disaster Management Authority & NDRF, Govt of India  
**Source**: NDMA Urban Search & Rescue & HAZMAT Guidelines  
**Statutory Shield**: Disaster Management Act 2005 & Section 134A MV Act 2019  

## Structural Building Collapse & Crush Injury (USAR §5)
- **Step 1: Scene Safety**: Check for gas leaks, secondary collapse, and electrical lines.
- **Step 2: Airway Protection**: Place wet cloth over victim's nose and mouth to prevent silica dust inhalation.
- **Step 3: Crush Syndrome Management**: For limbs compressed > 1 hour, prepare IV saline hydration before lifting debris to prevent acute reperfusion shock.
- **Step 4: Spinal Extrication**: Use rigid backboards and in-line spinal stabilization.
- **Contraindications**: Do not abruptly release prolonged limb crush without medical readiness.

## LPG & Chemical Gas Leak Safety
- **Step 1: Upwind Evacuation**: Move everyone upwind and crosswind immediately.
- **Step 2: No Electrical Switches**: Strictly do NOT touch light switches, appliances, or mobile phones.
- **Step 3: Damp Cloth Barrier**: Breathe through a damp cloth covering nose and mouth.
- **Step 4: Perimeter Control**: Isolate perimeter to at least 100 meters and call Fire 101.
- **Contraindications**: No open flames, matches, or electrical sparks.
