# AIIMS National Poisons Information Centre Emergency Protocols

**Authority**: AIIMS National Poisons Information Centre & MoHFW, Govt of India  
**Source**: NPIC Emergency Toxicology Manual  
**Statutory Shield**: Section 134A Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019  

## Corrosive & Chemical Ingestion
- **Step 1: Never Induce Vomiting**: Absolutely do NOT administer salt water or induce vomiting. Corrosives and hydrocarbons cause fatal airway destruction and aspiration if regurgitated.
- **Step 2: Decontamination**: Rinse oral cavity with cool water and spit out. Strip poison-contaminated clothing and wash skin with soap and water for 15 minutes.
- **Step 3: Sample Preservation**: Bring original chemical container or photograph label for ED identification.
- **Step 4: Recovery Positioning**: Place victim in lateral recovery position and dispatch 108 ALS.
- **Contraindications**: Do not attempt chemical neutralization (e.g. acids with baking soda or alkalis with vinegar); exothermic reactions cause deep thermal necrosis.
