# Screen Instruction: R3 — Full-Screen Urgent Emergency Alert (Incoming Call Style)

> **Screen ID**: `R3_RESPONDER_INCOMING_ALERT`  
> **Persona**: Community Medical Responder  
> **UI Type**: Full-Screen Urgent Modal Activity (Wakes Lock Screen & Bypasses DND)  
> **File/Composable Name**: `ResponderEmergencyAlertActivity.kt`  
> **Route**: Invoked via High-Priority Full-Screen Notification Intent  

---

## 1. Purpose & UX Objective
When an emergency occurs nearby, the responder's phone must sound an unignorable, urgent alarm—similar to an incoming telephone call. The screen displays the critical incident details (Distance, ETA, AI symptom summary, Severity badge, and required skills) and provides a **30-second decision timer** with large `[ ACCEPT / RESPOND ]` vs `[ DECLINE / PASS ]` buttons.

## 3. Component Details & Styling

### 3.1 Urgent Audio & OS Integration
* **Android Intent**: `setShowWhenLocked(true)`, `setTurnScreenOn(true)`, `NotificationManager.IMPORTANCE_HIGH`.
* **Audio**: Urgent alternating medical siren (`R.raw.emergency_alert_siren.wav`).
* **Haptics**: Continuous heavy vibrating pattern.

### 3.2 30-Second Countdown Timer
* Center circular progress indicator with 30s timer.
* Auto-Timeout: If 30 seconds elapse without responder action, the alert times out, dismisses, and the backend dispatch engine immediately pings the next closest volunteer.

### 3.3 Incident Briefing Card
* **Severity Header**: Level 5 (Dark Red `#B71C1C`) with flashing beacon.
* **Proximity & ETA**: `📍 650 meters away • ~3 mins by Scooter` (18sp, Bold, Inter).
* **AI Clinical Summary**: High-contrast, concise paragraph generated by Gemini 2.5 triage.
* **Required Skills Badge**: `CPR / Defibrillator Certified`.

### 3.4 Action Buttons
* **Accept Button (Green)**:
  * Text: `[ 🟢 I'M RESPONDING / ACCEPT ]`
  * Dimensions: Full Width, **$72\text{dp}$ Height**.
  * Color: Safe Green (`#2E7D32`), 18sp Bold White Text.
  * Haptic Feedback: Heavy confirm click.
* **Decline Button (Gray)**:
  * Text: `[ ⚪ Decline / Pass Alert ]`
  * Height: 48dp, Outlined Button (`#757575`).

---

## 4. State Management & Navigation
* **On Accept**:
  * Emits WebSocket `ACCEPT_DISPATCH`.
  * Navigates directly to **`R4: Response Accepted & Route Lock Screen`**.
* **On Decline**:
  * Opens **`R-P1: Decline Reason Modal Pop-up`**.
